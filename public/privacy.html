Privacy

Chronicle is a static site with no user accounts. The site stores your theme, reader mode and reading position in your browser’s local storage. Those preferences are not sent to a Chronicle application server.

Requests made by your browser

The homepage loads GoatCounter analytics from gc.zgo.at and sends page-view requests to chronicle.goatcounter.com. Article and repository images may load from their publishers’ servers. These third parties receive network information such as your IP address. Image requests use a no-referrer policy. Opening an external link takes you to that publisher’s site and its own policies.

Fonts and app assets are hosted with Chronicle. A service worker may cache pages and feeds for offline reading. Clear site data in your browser to remove local preferences, reading history and cached content. A cached edition retains its original content time.

Content processing

The scheduled builder retrieves public source material. It may send public titles and descriptions to the configured classification providers, and public article URLs to Jina Reader. It does not receive your personal reading preferences. Correction reports submitted on GitHub follow GitHub’s privacy settings and may be public.

Contact the maintainer about privacy.
