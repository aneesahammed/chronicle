No individual article corrections are recorded here yet. Report a content error with the story URL, edition date, disputed text and evidence.

Method changes in this version

Duplicate coverage is described as “seen on” feeds. Publication and discovery dates are distinguished. Failed refreshes retain the last successful content time. Learning descriptions are parsed from YouTube’s media fields, and source contribution is measured separately from reachability. Historical snapshots may contain older assessment metadata.

For each future article correction, this log will record the edition, affected item, what changed and the supporting source. Corrections should also update the current item and invalidate its cached excerpt where relevant.
