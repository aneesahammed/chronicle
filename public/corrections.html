Method changes

2026-10-29
  • Repeated links are shown as “seen on N feeds”, not as corroboration.
  • Publication and discovery dates are shown separately.
  • A failed refresh keeps the time of the last successful edition.
  • Learning descriptions are read from YouTube’s media fields.
  • Reachable sources and sources with fresh items are counted separately.

Archived editions from before a change may carry older assessment details.
